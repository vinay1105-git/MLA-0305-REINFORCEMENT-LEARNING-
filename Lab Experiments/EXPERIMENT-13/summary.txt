=========================================================
EXPERIMENT 13: DEEP DETERMINISTIC POLICY GRADIENT (DDPG)
=========================================================

1. AIM:
   To implement Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG) algorithm
   for continuous action-space control problems.

2. PROCEDURE:
   - Construct Deterministic Actor pi(s|theta) and Action-Value Critic Q(s, a|phi).
   - Implement Experience Replay Buffer and continuous action exploration noise.
   - Update Critic via TD-error and Actor via Deterministic Policy Gradient Theorem.
   - Softly update target networks (theta' <- tau*theta + (1-tau)*theta') for tracking stability.

3. KEY TAKEAWAYS:
   - DDPG extends DQN principles to continuous control domains efficiently.
   - Soft target network updates prevent deadly divergence and optimization instability.
