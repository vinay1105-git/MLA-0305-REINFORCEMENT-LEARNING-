=========================================================
EXPERIMENT 15: MODEL-BASED RL (DYNA-Q IMPLEMENTATION)
=========================================================

1. AIM:
   To implement Dyna-Q, combining direct Reinforcement Learning
   with Model Learning and simulated background planning updates.

2. PROCEDURE:
   - Execute real environment step and update Q-Table directly.
   - Update transition model M(s, a) -> (r, s') using real experience.
   - Perform 'n' background planning steps by sampling previous state-action pairs from M.

3. KEY TAKEAWAYS:
   - Model-based planning significantly reduces required real-world environmental interactions.
   - Accelerated learning speed enables fast policy convergence in sample-constrained scenarios.
