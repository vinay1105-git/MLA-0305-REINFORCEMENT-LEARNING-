=========================================================
EXPERIMENT 9: DEEP Q-NETWORK (DQN) IMPLEMENTATION
=========================================================

1. AIM:
   To implement Deep Q-Networks (DQN) combining Q-Learning with
   discretized state-space function approximation and Experience Replay.

2. PROCEDURE:
   - Discretize continuous state vectors into state space buckets.
   - Store trajectories (S, A, R, S', Done) inside Experience Replay Buffer.
   - Sample transitions to decorrelate updates and update action values.

3. KEY TAKEAWAYS:
   - Experience Replay breaks temporal correlation for stable policy learning.
   - Episode rewards steadily vary upwards as exploration transitions to exploitation.
