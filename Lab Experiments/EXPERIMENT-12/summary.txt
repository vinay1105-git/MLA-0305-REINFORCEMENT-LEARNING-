=========================================================
EXPERIMENT 12: PROXIMAL POLICY OPTIMIZATION (PPO)
=========================================================

1. AIM:
   To implement Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) using a Clipped
   Surrogate Objective function to enforce stable policy updates.

2. PROCEDURE:
   - Collect state trajectories under the old policy parameters pi_old.
   - Calculate probability ratios r_t(theta) = pi_theta(a|s) / pi_old(a|s).
   - Compute clipped objective: min(r_t * A_t, clip(r_t, 1-eps, 1+eps) * A_t).
   - Perform multiple minibatch optimization epochs per trajectory.

3. KEY TAKEAWAYS:
   - Clipping prevents destructively large policy updates, maintaining monotonicity.
   - Enables sample-efficient multi-epoch training over single trajectory batches.
