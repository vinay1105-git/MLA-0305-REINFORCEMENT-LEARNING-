=========================================================
EXPERIMENT 14: SOFT ACTOR-CRITIC (SAC IMPLEMENTATION)
=========================================================

1. AIM:
   To implement Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) utilizing Maximum Entropy RL
   framework to optimize expected return and policy entropy concurrently.

2. PROCEDURE:
   - Construct Twin Q-Critics Q1(s,a), Q2(s,a) and stochastic Soft Actor pi(a|s).
   - Formulate Soft State-Value targets incorporating entropy term: - alpha * log(pi).
   - Update Twin Critics via MSE loss and Policy via KL-divergence minimization.

3. KEY TAKEAWAYS:
   - Entropy regularization encourages aggressive exploration and prevents early policy collapse.
   - Twin Q-Functions successfully eliminate value overestimation bias.
