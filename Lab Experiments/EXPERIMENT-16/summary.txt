=========================================================
EXPERIMENT 16: MULTI-AGENT RL (INDEPENDENT Q-LEARNING)
=========================================================

1. AIM:
   To implement Independent Q-Learning (IQL) for Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning (MARL)
   in a cooperative gridworld task with shared collision interactions.

2. PROCEDURE:
   - Instantiate separate independent Q-tables for Agent 1 and Agent 2.
   - Execute simultaneous environment steps and evaluate joint transition rewards.
   - Update each agent's individual policy independently assuming stationary environments.

3. KEY TAKEAWAYS:
   - IQL provides decentralization with zero inter-agent communication overhead.
   - Handles non-stationarity in multi-agent settings to reach stable joint policies.
